Strategy Tips

• Attack from multiple sides for flanking bonuses; surround an enemy for devastating encirclement damage.

• Keep units near friendly cities for supply — cut-off units lose morale and strength.

• Race rivals to build a World Wonder for a permanent empire-wide edge.

• Your National Doctrine shapes everything — play to its strengths.

• Assign Generals to your best units; they grow stronger with every battle.
